About the Brand

Almera began with the friendship of two women — Alexandra and Maria — and a shared idea that, over time, grew into a jewelry brand. At its heart is a simple philosophy: jewelry should be part of everyday life, not something reserved for a special occasion.

01 — Alexandra

For Alexandra, her love of jewelry began in childhood. She collected stones, admired jewelry, and was fascinated by its shapes, colors, and intricate details. Over the years, this curiosity grew into a genuine passion for the art of jewelry and a desire to one day create something of her own.

02 — Maria

For Maria, Armenia became an important part of her life — a country where she lived for many years. It was there that she experienced a culture in which jewelry holds a special place in everyday life. In Armenia, jewelry does not have to wait for a special occasion — it becomes part of the way women dress every day, a way to express femininity, individuality, and self-appreciation. There is a love for dressing beautifully, paying attention to detail, and wearing fine jewelry not only when going out, but simply because beauty can be part of every day.

This is where two stories came together around one shared idea, giving rise to Almera — jewelry that becomes part of your everyday life.

03 — Certification

Quality Verified by Experts

Every diamond undergoes an independent gemological assessment. Certification by GIA, IGI, and NGTC confirms the stated characteristics and quality of each stone.
